WHO WE ARE

Small Actions.
Lasting Impact.

EverGreen Global Initiative (EGI) is a youth-driven initiative established in 2026 with a vision to inspire people to take meaningful action for a greener and more sustainable future.

We bring together students, youth, volunteers, professionals and communities to transform environmental awareness into practical action.

From planting trees and conserving water to promoting climate action, environmental awareness and sustainable agriculture, EGI believes that meaningful change begins when people choose to act.
